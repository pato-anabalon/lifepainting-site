## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript strict, Tailwind CSS 4, GSAP, Framer Motion, Vercel + GitHub (confirmed by the owner in the project brief).

## Users
Auckland homeowners (North Shore and Auckland Central first) planning a repaint, renovation or new-home finish, who want to deal directly with the person doing the work. They are not painting experts: they need to understand what each service includes and feel safe contacting a painter. Secondary and not promoted: builders who occasionally subcontract.

## Product Purpose
Life Painting & Decorating is an Auckland painting company (interior, exterior, decorative finishes, preparation and repairs incl. GIB stopping and skimming). The site exists to turn visits into well-informed quote enquiries. Success = a clear enquiry with service, suburb and scope.

## Positioning
Premium workmanship, approachable service: careful preparation and attention to detail, direct communication with the owners, and a promise to do what they say. "Premium" refers to finish quality, never luxury pricing. Mid/premium price level. Painting first; decorating secondary.

## Operating Context
Founded 2023 by a married couple; owners' earlier industry experience must not be presented as company age. Leads arrive by phone (027 375 9093) and email (contact@lifepaintingdec.co.nz). Service areas: Auckland, North Shore, Auckland Central, shown generically with no street address. Exterior work is "designed for Auckland conditions".

## Capabilities and Constraints
Services: interior painting, exterior painting (weatherboards, masonry/brickwork, roofs, fences), decorative finishes (textures, feature walls, special finishes), preparation & repairs (GIB stopping, skimming, surface prep, minor repairs). No pricing or plans published. Copy is en-NZ. Domain: lifepaintingdec.co.nz. The owner (Patricio) makes all commits and pushes.

## Brand Commitments
Name shown: Life Painting (legal: Life Painting & Decorating). Slogan: "Good preparations. Better Finishes." Essence: Care. Craft. Reliability (pillar: Reliable). Values: Reliability, Craftsmanship, Care, Attention to detail, Communication. Direction: "Old-school standards. New-school ambition." Traditional, premium, bold, personal, different, reliable; never outdated, pretentious, childish, informal or eccentric. Visual world is mid-century (1940s-60s) commercial signage with the brand guidelines' palette (Cream #F8F0DA, Dark Turquoise #1F4A3A, Gray Azure #5A9CAB, Yellow #FDD01D, Mustard #EAA72F), Lovelo / Montserrat type, and logos in public/logos. New Zealand connection stays subtle: no kiwis or ferns. Binding (owner-confirmed): the owner's hard offset "sign-painter" shadows and geometric motifs stay; stricter anti-ornament rules from design skills apply elsewhere (hybrid).

## Evidence on Hand
Brand Discovery Questionnaire, Brand Guidelines PDF and client info PDF in public/docs; logo files in public/logos. Absent and must not be fabricated: client photos, testimonials, reviews, awards, metrics, case studies, certifications, licences, insurance, guarantees. Open-licence photographs of Auckland may be used as credited ambient context only, never as Life Painting's own work.

## Product Principles
1. Reliability is proven through process and clarity, not claims.
2. Preparation is the story: the finish is won before the paint.
3. Help a non-expert decide which service they need before they contact.
4. Never present invented or borrowed evidence as the company's own.
5. Warm, direct, human en-NZ voice; premium means careful, not expensive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigable, visible focus, sufficient contrast (azure only for decorative/large type on cream), reduced-motion alternatives, touch targets of 44px or more.
